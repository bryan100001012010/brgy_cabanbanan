How it works

The Katarungang Pambarangay process

Most disputes between residents are first brought to the barangay for mediation before they can be filed in court. Here's what happens after you submit a complaint.

1

Complaint is logged

Your report is recorded and given a tracking number for reference.

2

Summons is issued

The Lupon Tagapamayapa sends a notice to the other party to appear for mediation.

3

Mediation hearing

Both parties meet with a barangay official or Lupon member to seek a settlement.

4

Settlement or certification

If unresolved, a Certificate to File Action is issued so the case can proceed to court.
